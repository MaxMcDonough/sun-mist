Go-to-Market Strategy & Timeline

Three-phase expansion from pilot to regional scale

Phase 1 — May to September 2026

Pilot & Proof of Concept

Location: Ocean City, MD (primary beach attraction, 8M+ annual visitors)

Tactics:

  • Deploy 2 operators during peak season (summer weekends + school vacations)
  • Local PR & influencer seeding (beach lifestyle content)
  • Hotel & rental partnerships for customer referrals
  • Beach festival activation & sponsorships

Success Metrics: 5,000+ applications, 500+ unique customers, 20%+ repeat rate, positive press

Phase 2 — 2027

Regional Expansion & Product Launch

Locations: Expand to Rehoboth Beach & Dewey Beach (2 new beaches)

Tactics:

  • Scale to 8 operators across 3 beaches
  • Launch mobile app (QR loyalty, scheduling, day passes)
  • Introduce family packs, day passes, loyalty rewards
  • Hotel partnerships (premium resort packages)
  • Paid social media (Instagram, TikTok targeting beach-goers)

Success Metrics: 50,000+ applications, $535K revenue, 25%+ repeat customer rate

Phase 3 — 2028+

Scale & Franchise Model

Locations: NJ Shore, Virginia Beach, Outer Banks (5+ locations)

Tactics:

  • Operator licensing / franchise model for rapid scaling
  • 20+ operators across 5 premium beach markets
  • Add adjacent products: after-sun care, cooling mist, insect repellent
  • Corporate partnerships (cruise lines, resort chains, water parks)
  • National brand campaigns & sponsorships

Success Metrics: 150,000+ applications, $1.34M revenue, path to 7-figure profitability

07

Implementation Strategy

Detailed operational blueprint for launch and scaling

⚙️ Equipment & Supplies

  • Misting System: 4L capacity backpack + fine-mist nozzle (~$300)
  • Sunscreen: FDA-compliant commercial formula, 200+ oz/day supply
  • Payment Terminal: Square/Stripe reader (~$50)
  • Uniform Kit: branded shirt, apron, accessories (~$150/operator)
  • Initial inventory: ~$1,200 startup cost per operator

👥 Hiring & Training

  • Recruitment: University partnerships, seasonal job boards
  • Training Program: 2-day onboarding covering:
  • — Application technique & coverage
  • — Customer service scripts
  • — Safety & liability protocols
  • — Payment processing
  • Quality Control: Weekly mystery shoppers + feedback

📋 Daily Operations

  • Pre-shift: Equipment check, supply loading, zone briefing
  • Coverage: Assigned beach zones with rotation schedule
  • Inventory: Daily tracking, restock protocols
  • Weather: Contingency plans for rain, extreme heat
  • Reporting: End-of-day numbers, customer feedback logged

⚖️ Regulatory & Compliance

  • FDA: Using pre-approved commercial sunscreen, not manufacturing
  • Licensing: Business license, beach vendor permit
  • Insurance: General liability + product liability coverage
  • Health Dept: Compliance with local beach regulations
  • Legal: Terms of service, liability waiver templates
