Do's & Don'ts

Do

Use primary colors to establish visual hierarchy and brand recognition. Combine gold with blue for maximum impact.

Don't

Avoid using all colors equally. Neutrals should dominate with primary colors as accents.

Do

Use Sea Glass and Sand for backgrounds. They provide calm, readable surfaces for content.

Don't

Don't use multiple secondary colors together. They can feel chaotic and unprofessional.

06 / Photography Direction

Photography Direction

A visual language guide for all photography. While Sun Mist uses minimal photography, when images are selected, they should follow this mood and aesthetic direction.

Golden Hour Vibes
Golden Hour Vibes
Warm, soft light with long shadows. Captured during late afternoon when sun is low and golden. Creates premium, aspirational feeling.
Clean & Bright
Clean & Bright Lifestyle
High-key, bright photography with minimal shadows. Fresh, airy, and accessible. Shows real people enjoying beach moments.
Action & Motion
Action Shots of Misting
Dynamic, in-motion photography showing the misting service. Captures convenience and joy. Movement suggests speed and ease.
Diverse Families & Groups
Inclusive & Diverse
Real people of all ages, backgrounds, and body types enjoying the beach. Authentic moments, genuine smiles, and diverse representation.

Photography Guidelines

Do

  • ✓ Shoot during golden hour or overcast days
  • ✓ Include real people and authentic moments
  • ✓ Show diverse ages, backgrounds, abilities
  • ✓ Capture action and movement
  • ✓ Use brand colors as accents in styling
  • ✓ Include natural beach elements

Don't

  • ✗ Overly polished or artificial looks
  • ✗ Dark, moody, or gloomy lighting
  • ✗ Overly white or homogeneous casting
  • ✗ Stock photography feel
  • ✗ Heavy filters or color grading
  • ✗ Cluttered or confusing compositions
07 / Brand Voice & Tone

Brand Voice & Tone

Sun Mist speaks with a voice that's friendly and confident, always making sun safety feel effortless. Helpful without being preachy, expert without being pretentious.

Voice Attributes

Friendly

Warm, approachable, and conversational. We talk like a trusted friend, not a corporation.

Confident

We know sun protection. We back it up with expertise, but never condescend.

Clean

Clear and direct. No jargon, no fluff. Simple words that make sense.

Effortless

We make sun safety convenient. Our copy reflects that ease and simplicity.

Tone Spectrum

Formal
Casual

Most Sun Mist communication sits comfortably in the middle-to-casual range. We're professional but not stuffy. Expert but approachable. Helpful without being preachy.

Do's & Don'ts

Do

"Mist. Move. Enjoy." — Short, punchy, action-oriented.

Don't

"Dermatologically advanced UV protection application service" — Too technical.

Do

"You're protected. Now go have fun." — Confident, permission-giving.

Don't

"Failure to apply adequate SPF may result in..." — Fear-based, clinical.

Do

"We'll be at your favorite beach spot at 10am" — Direct, helpful, clear.

Don't

"Our bespoke solar protection dispensing unit shall commence operations..." — Overly formal.

Sample Copy

Homepage Hero
"Sunscreen that comes to you. Professional-grade protection, zero hassle. More beach time, less reapplication anxiety."

"Reapply every two hours? We've got you. Book a misting break and get back to what matters — making memories at the beach. ☀️"

"Hey! I'm with Sun Mist. Are you all set for sun protection? I can give you a quick refresh right now—takes about 30 seconds and you're good to go."
