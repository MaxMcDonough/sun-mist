01 — What This Is

Sunscreen, simplified.

Turning sunscreen into a service, not a product.

Sun Mist is a mobile, on-demand sunscreen application service designed specifically for high-traffic beaches.

Trained operators walk designated beach areas equipped with lightweight backpack misting systems, offering fast, clean SPF application to beachgoers.

Each application takes 10–20 seconds, delivering even coverage without physical contact.

10–20s per application  ·  Even coverage  ·  No contact

The service eliminates:

✕ Carrying sunscreen
✕ Reapplying manually
✕ Using messy or expired products
